I work on modeling gravitational waveforms from binary black hole systems with eccentric orbits.

I also work on the structure and stability of differentially rotating neutron stars as realistic post-merger remnants of core-collapse supernovae or binay neutron star mergers.

Outside of research, I mostly like exploring new places and discussing cool astronomy topics with beginners.
